What I’ve Been Reading/Watching/Hearing:

  • King’s Avatar (TV show, Netflix) – as I’ve been trying to get into Chinese, I’ve been watching TV shows in Chinese. This one is about a professional gamer who gets kicked out of his team because they want him to become a celebrity and he wants to remain anonymous. So he goes across the road to an internet cafe and forms his own team.
  • ChinesePod (podcast, Spotify) – 15-20 minute episodes with a short Chinese dialogue, and then some explanation and translation in a mixture of English and Chinese. At different levels (beginner, pre-intermediate, intermediate, upper-intermediate, etc.). I find I’m around pre-intermediate to intermediate.
  • Chinese Cooking Demystified (Youtube channel) – I never learned to cook Chinese food, and now I can, via Youtube! This is a husband-wife duo, their episodes are suitably short and to the point, and they include recipes in the descriptions.
  • What Colour is your Parachute (book) – This is a famous book about how to find a good job for you. I remember I read it back in 2015 when I was desperately trying to find a job. I had positive memories of it, but upon re-reading it I actually don’t think it’s very useful. It focuses too much on finding a job which is a perfect fit for your unique traits. Especially when you’re quite junior you don’t have that many valuable unique traits! Some of the advice in the first few chapters about how to job search and apply are helpful though.
