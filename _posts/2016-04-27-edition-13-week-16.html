Went Bushwalking

The reason for this late post is that I did a 3-day solo hike this long weekend! It was a 46 km trek from Katoomba to the Jenolan Caves. It was a totally spontaneous decision to walk from Wednesday, but I was able to buy a tent, sleeping bag, sleeping mat and borrow a sleeping bag in time.

The weather was perfect for a hike, and I met some cool people along the trip.

Some of you may know that I do a bit of coding in my spare time. Well, recently, I’ve been able to try out my luck with using Python (a language I’ve learned at home) for Big Data purposes – mainly data scraping from websites.

Can We Have Faith in Politics?

I went to a dinner where Bay Warburton spoke about how his faith shapes his work in politics. I find it interesting to understand how people’s faith and values shape their world view, given that I was brought up at an Anglican school and have with Anglican choirs, but don’t feel very religious at all.

The speech was fine, although Warburton’s main point was that being Christian meant diligence and hard work in the job, which wasn’t much to reflect about.
