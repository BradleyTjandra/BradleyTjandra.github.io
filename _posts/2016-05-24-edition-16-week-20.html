Melbourne Uni makes everyone studying Accounting subjects

http://farragomagazine.com/2016/05/16/spot-the-difference/

The changes would see a reduction in the maximum number of points students can get from breadth subjects, from 100 to 75 points [BT: that means only 6 breadth subjects over 3 years, rather than 8].

The reduction of breadth points is to allow for two more core subjects: Accounting Reports and Analysis and a new subject, Principles of Finance, which will replace Finance 1. The aim of the change is to better prepare students for the workforce.

Re: Accounting Reports and Analysis, I think it is a good move. People need to read financial statements, even though it seems like a boring topic.

It limits the number of breadths. Breadths are a wonderful chance for students to genuinely learn something new, something few people have the time to do post-uni. That is a shame, and inherent to 3-year-undergrad nature of BCom.
