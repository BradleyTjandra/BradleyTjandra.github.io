2. Unz on Race/IQ – The Rural/Urban Divide

The importance of urbanisation of the increase of IQ, which seems to counter the argument that genetics is important for IQ. At least, to me, it suggests that the most IQ gains to be made are improving standard of living of those in rural/poorer areas. But are there still genetic effects looking at urbanised people?

Also:

[The IQ of East Asian populations], seem almost entirely unaffected by even the most massive trends of urbanization. This has led me to suggest that for some unknown, possibly biological reason, East Asians can achieve nearly their full IQ potential without requiring the same beneficial “cognitive development environment” which white Europeans seem to require.
