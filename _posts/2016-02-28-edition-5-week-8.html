What I’ve been Reading/Watching/Hearing:

  • How to Email (website) – a self-written article summarising what we’ve learned about writing emails. I’d love to share in further detail the 2.5-hour presentation we got.
  • Creating Behaviour Change (website) – I’m becoming interested in marketing. This website is a really clean framework for how to improve it.
  • How Different Companies Deal With Your Data (podcast) – FiveThirtyEight takes us through how companies are working to protect your data from the Government and/or how it fits into their revenue model.
  • Letters from a Stoic (book – techically, letters) – an inspiring read about how to be content, what is worth pursuing, what is worth stressing over (nothing), and how to be proud of your life. Much recommended so far. Stoicism is a mindset I feel would have been very useful for me circa year 9-10.
