What I’m reading/watching/hearing:

  • The Economics of Sleep (podcast) – Dubner looks at how much sleep we are getting, and whether there’s any real benefit behind them. In short, yes. One of Freakonomic’s most listened to podcasts.
  • Islam and the Future of Tolerance: A Dialogue (book) – A former-Islamist-now-just-a-Muslim (Maajid Nawaz) and an atheist (Sam Harris) talk about the problem of Jihadism and the threat that Islam could face both for itself and for the wider community. A great short book, and good nuanced conversation. It suggests to me that there’s too much focus on fear or tolerance, and not enough on critical thinking.
  • A variety of papers about empathy and animals – I was interested in reading about why children like animals, given children can’t perspective take until their older. I’ve downloaded what I’ve been reading and I have put them at the link. No copyright infringment intended.
