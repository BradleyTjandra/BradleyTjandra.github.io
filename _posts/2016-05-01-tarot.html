Wands -> Microphones. Microphones represent the willingness to be the centre of attention, to excite everyone with ideas, to bring a crowd together. It’s the hip hop artist rapping through their mike, or the politician at the podium.

Swords -> Pens. Ideas come into being through the pen. The consultant at a whiteboard, the artist at an easel, the scientist writing down ideas. The heavy handed criticism of Andrew Bolt.

Pentacles -> Messages. Messages and conversations are how business gets done these days. We’re in the information economy.

King: Action under this suit. Control, mastery, good execution. The ultimate authority and giver of validation.

Queen: Feelings under this suit. What happens when people are happy and content with this suit.

Jack: Excess under this suit. Ambitious and complete immersion, burning bridges, potentially.

Page: Curiosity under this suit. First experiences with this. Also complete immersion, but not deep. The only thing in this person’s world is this suit, because it’s so interesting.

Ace: The beginning of a new chapter. With much fanfare, this suit becomes a main focus.

Ten: The end of the story. You’ve completed this chapter, and achieved what you would want under this suit.
