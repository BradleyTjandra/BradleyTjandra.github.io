Actually working on stuff starts next week.

New Friends

Effective Altruism Meetup

I went to a meetup with other people interested in Effective Altruism. I mainly listened to people speak – which was very engaging and I learned a lot – but I need to get better at discussing ideas off-the-cuff.

Food

Signal

I’m trying to get my privacy-concerned friends to download Signal for Android and iPhone.  What does it do?

Signal uses an advanced end to end encryption protocol that provides privacy for every message every time.

What I’m reading/watching/hearing:

  • Deadpool (film) – A regular amusing film. It will probably appeal to the people who want to see it, but I wouldn’t consider it necessary.
  • How to Travel (article) – 21 quick, contrarian tips on how to travel which rejects a lot of the standard methods of travel.
  • Emerson on Travel (article) – A brief essay about why travel is often foolish and unfulfilling.
  • Less Wrong (website) – A huge collection of articles about how to think more rationally and realise mistakes your brain makes. Written in a very clear and helpful way.
  • Eric Weinstein on Challenging “Reality,” Working with Peter Thiel, and Destroying Education to Save It (podcast) – A long podcast, but it covers many contrarian opinions by a man who believes in the need for contrarian thinking.
  • Rule-breaking in children predicts future success (article) – A brief summary of a new, interesting study. Preliminary findings.
