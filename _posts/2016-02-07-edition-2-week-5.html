What I’m reading/watching/hearing:

  1. The Signal and the Noise (book) – explores what the world of data is now capable of, and what it is not capable of, using many case studies which help to illustrate the general usefulness of them.
  2. The Internet’s Own Boy (doco) – the programmer-activist-entrepreneur guy who was “targetted” by some governmental bodies and, facing potentially 35 years in prison, killed himself; very important discussion about governments using data, governments being transparent with using data, and with how much/little control we have over the laws which govern us.
  3. Values and Frames (website) – summarising research from the past few decades, this explains how our values impact what marketing messages are important; this is revealing from a psychology/sociology angle.
