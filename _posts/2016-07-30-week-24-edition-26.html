What I’ve been reading/hearing/watching:

  • Mr Robot_2.0 (TV) – I don’t watch many TV shows, but when I do, I watch Mr Robot. If you haven’t watched it yet and you like conspiracy, fight-club-esque films then you should.
  • Superintelligence (book)  – About Artificial Intelligence: how close we are, how it could work, and why people are worried
  • 80,000 hours (website) – How to find/choose/get a career which you will actually like. If you don’t know what you’re doing with your (professional) life, start here: We reviewed over 60 studies about what makes for a dream job. Here’s what we found.
  • Population ethics (idea) – “Is it possible to make the world a better place by creating additional happy people?” If you think more happiness is always more moral than less happiness, then you think we should have lots of kids. It’s an interesting idea, but it’s even more interesting to try to explain it!
    • (As an aside: If you think happiness is not always more moral than less happiness, then what exactly is your sense of morality based on?)
