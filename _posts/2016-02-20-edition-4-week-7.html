Working

Work has been good. Everything is done on a project basis, and your main goal is around billable hours – so everyone is working on many projects at a time, and they need to record how much time they spend on each project. I didn’t have a lot of work this week, so I didn’t rack up too many hours but I think that’s inevitable.

Work goals

So far I’m on a lot of very classic general insurance projects. I enjoy seeing the intricacies of the individual projects (e.g. medical indemnity), but I think I also want to get involved in more programming: more R and big data stuff.

Managing time

I’ve found your spare time slips out of your fingers a lot more easily when you’re working and using your brain. I am trying to find a way to manage my many ambitions outside of work with work, and with my need to relax. Any hints are welcome.
