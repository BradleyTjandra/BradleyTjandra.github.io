Climbed the Blue Mountains

Went to the Blue Mountains and did the Wentworth trail. It was perfect weather for it, and we managed to walk the 3-5 hour trail in 2.5 hours. Here is a selfie and a waterfall. Shout out to WordPress for automattic (badum-ch) layout.

Trip to Melbourne

Caught up with many people from Melbourne. Won’t detail everyone because it’s both uninteresting and probably someone people want it to be private. Recommended eats, however:

  • Nishikian serves great Japanese food. Lots of sashimi, but many other dishes. Yum yum yum.
  • Vapiano moderately priced Italian food. Already popular in Sydney, although there’s probably some small Italian restaurant on Lygon with better value per $.
  • 8 Days mainly brunch food
